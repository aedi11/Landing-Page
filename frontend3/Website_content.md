SECTION 3: THE ENGINE (Core Capabilities)
AI-Enabled Automated Tool Kit
AEDI offers a comprehensive generative AI approach to synthesize and deliver physically feasible, comprehensive, embedded system designs.

Manufacturing-Ready Solutions: AEDI computes and synthesizes input options to provide optimized system design solutions.

End-to-End Automation: Our toolkit delivers automated simulation, schematic creation, PCB routing and generation, multiple design options, and BOM generation.

Precision and Reliability: We utilize causal reasoning to eliminate hallucinations in design.

Comprehensive Features: Capabilities span architecture and IC selection, firmware development, physics-based schematic design and validation, cost estimation, and safety verification.

SECTION 4: THE SCOPE (Applications)
One Platform. Infinite Embedded Systems.
AEDI acts as a comprehensive dynamic foundational model capable of generating a spectrum of application-level embedded system designs.

Battery & Mobility Systems: We automate mechanical, thermal, and electronic (BMS) battery designs for portable packs, two-wheelers, and three-wheelers.

Aerospace & Defence: Our designs power drones, night vision powering solutions, and radar systems.

Automotive Integration: We build complex controllers for ECU load balancing and optimization, as well as electric vehicle chargers.

Consumer & Power Electronics: Our solutions extend to motor controllers, home electronics, and portable audio devices.

SECTION 5: THE IMPACT (Value Proposition)
Redefining Development Timelines
Our new-tech enabled system delivers best-in-class optimized system solutions.

Massive Time Savings: Experience a greater than 60% reduction in design and iteration times.

Engineering Optimization: AEDI drives up to a 70% reduction in engineering efforts.

Seamless Manufacturing: We deliver custom firmware, precise Gerber files, and an optimized Bill of Materials (BOM) for effective sourcing and seamless assembly.
